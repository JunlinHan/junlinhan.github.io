Profile

Welcome to my website.

  • I am a Member of Technical Staff at AMI Labs, where I work on pre-training and the capabilities of world models.
  • I did my PhD in the Torr Vision Group at the University of Oxford, advised by Prof. Philip Torr. During my PhD, I spent three years at Meta (FAIR and GenAI) as a joint PhD student working on multimodal pre-training, MLLMs, and 3D/video generation with Dr. Mike Lewis and Dr. Filippos Kokkinos.
  • I received my BSc from the Australian National University, where I was fortunate to be supervised by Dr. Lars Petersson, Prof. Hongdong Li, and Prof. Ian Reid.

Research Interests

My research focuses on multimodal foundation models, including multimodal large language models and generative models, as well as their interactions.

I am particularly interested in multimodal approaches to advanced intelligence, especially how vision can contribute to its development through large-scale pre-training.

Multimodal pre-training
Selected projects: Physics of multimodal pretraining, Visual priors in LLM pre-training, Beyond language modeling
Multimodal post-training
Selected projects: Llama 4, Long video understanding
Generative models, especially 3D and video
Selected projects: Scaling up 3D generation, High-quality 3D asset generation, Scaling up neural rendering
